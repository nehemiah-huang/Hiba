My thought Process

Structured in Thinking,
Adaptive in Execution

My work focuses on bringing clarity and structure to complex environments. I collaborate with founders and small teams where creativity, operations, and strategy must move together.

What shapes my work

From Systems to Strategy

I do not believe in processes that look impressive but make everyday work harder. The best systems are the ones people can actually use. They make decisions clearer, communication easier, and growth more sustainable.

I am drawn to businesses that are growing quickly, becoming more layered, or struggling to keep pace with their own complexity.

I began my career in product support within a technology environment, working closely with product managers and engineers on a large operational platform. That experience exposed me to the mechanics of systems at scale and sharpened my interest in the processes, workflows, and decisions that sit behind the visible side of a business.

Since then, I have worked across a wide range of environments, from technology platforms to creative and experiential spaces, across different industries and global markets.

My approach is grounded in simplification. I assess systems by breaking them down to their foundations, identifying redundancies, replacing tools that create more work than they solve, and introducing ways of working that are easier to follow and more sustainable over time.

That is also why I am increasingly interested in the thoughtful use of AI, automation, and digital tools. Not because every business needs more technology, but because the right technology, used intentionally, can reduce friction, improve visibility, and make work feel lighter. Increasingly, I see my work evolving at the intersection of operations, systems, and intelligent tools.

Curiosity remains central to how I work. Each project is an opportunity to understand a new environment, uncover what sits beneath the surface, and turn complexity into something clear, functional, and quietly effective.

How I Typically Support

The Way We Work Together

The way we work together depends on what you are building and where you are in that journey. In some cases, you may need a short period of strategic clarity. In others, you may need someone more closely embedded in the day-to-day rhythm of the work.

01

Consultant

I step in to assess your systems, workflows, or ongoing projects and bring clarity to how things are structured. This often involves identifying operational gaps, refining how things are done, and helping you see where adjustments can create meaningful improvements.

02

Embedded Operator

I work within your business to help manage the operational side of it. This can involve coordinating projects, structuring workflows, and ensuring that ideas move forward with consistent execution.

03

Founder's Right Hand

The work becomes more integrated. I work directly alongside you to help carry the operational weight of the business so you can focus more fully on vision, relationships, and growth — managing priorities, following through on decisions, and handling the moving parts that would otherwise sit with you.

04

Special Initiatives Lead

At times, I also take on responsibility for leading specific initiatives, particularly when a project or focus area requires careful coordination across collaborators, timelines, and moving parts

05

Strategic Partner

The collaboration becomes more shared. I work alongside you or your team to help shape and build an initiative from an early stage, contributing to direction, structure, and execution as the idea develops.
